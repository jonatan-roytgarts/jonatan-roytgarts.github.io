Perspective

Security begins with understanding the system.

My work sits at the intersection of cybersecurity, investigation, engineering, and systems thinking. I am interested not only in detecting that something happened, but in understanding how events connect, why systems fail, and how technical evidence can become an explainable story.

Security produces enormous amounts of data. The useful part is finding the evidence that actually changes our understanding.

Selected Work

One project. One problem worth solving.

Incident Loom is where I am developing and demonstrating my current approach to security analysis, software design, telemetry normalization, evidence correlation, and explainable incident reconstruction.

Security Investigation Platform

Incident
Loom

A vendor-neutral security investigation project designed to transform fragmented telemetry into an explainable attack timeline, probable event chain, MITRE ATT&CK mappings, and confidence-aware conclusions.

  • Normalize heterogeneous security events into a common internal model.
  • Correlate evidence across endpoint, identity, network, cloud, and other telemetry.
  • Preserve the reasoning behind conclusions rather than producing unexplained alerts.
  • Help an investigator move from many disconnected events toward one coherent incident narrative.
